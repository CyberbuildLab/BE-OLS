How scores work
  • Connectivity (0 to 3): links to an upper vocabulary, a BE-OLS ontology and a meta schema.
  • Accessibility (0 to 3): conceptual model, serialization, HTTP(S) IRI.
  • Documentation & Reuse (0 to 2): documentation link, rdfs:comment coverage.
  • Governance (0 to 3): license, version, creator.

Best-practice items do not affect the score.
